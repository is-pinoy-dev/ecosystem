Before you create your subdomain JSON file, you need your DNS values from your hosting provider:

1. **CNAME value** — a hostname your provider gives you (e.g. `xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx.vercel-dns-017.com.`)
2. **TXT verification value** — a domain-ownership challenge. Required on Vercel; not used by GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.

## What each provider needs

| Provider | CNAME | TXT verification | Cloudflare proxy |
|----------|-------|------------------|------------------|
| [Vercel](/guides/providers/vercel) | Project-specific `*.vercel-dns-*.com` target | **Always required** | Leave off |
| [GitHub Pages](/guides/providers/github-pages) | `your-username.github.io.` | Not supported | Leave off |
| [Cloudflare Pages](/guides/providers/cloudflare-pages) | `your-project.pages.dev.` | Not needed | Must stay off |

<Callout type="info">
  More provider guides (Netlify and others) will be added in the future. If your platform isn't listed, look for a "Custom Domain" or "Domains" settings page in your provider's dashboard — it will show you a CNAME or A record to configure. Note that only Vercel's DNS-based verification is supported today; if your platform requires a TXT challenge of its own, open an issue on the [domains repository](https://github.com/is-pinoy-dev/domains/issues).
</Callout>
