## 1. Overview

is-pinoy.dev is a free subdomain service operated by the **is-pinoy-dev** open source community on GitHub. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)** and its implementing rules.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a data subject.

## 2. What We Collect

When you register a subdomain, we collect only the information you voluntarily submit via a GitHub pull request:

- Your **GitHub username**
- Your chosen **subdomain name**
- Your **DNS records** (the targets your subdomain points to)
- The **date** of your registration
- Your **email address** *(optional — only if you choose to provide it; if included, it will be **publicly visible** in the repository)*

If you enable the **Contact Form** tool (see Section 5b), we additionally store your **GitHub account email** as the delivery address for messages visitors submit — this is kept **privately** in our database and is never published to the repository, unlike the optional `owner.email` field above.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or any other personal data beyond what is described in this policy.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

- Provision and manage your subdomain in our DNS configuration
- Identify your registration for support and abuse resolution
- Maintain a public record of registered subdomains in the open source repository
- Contact you regarding your subdomain (only if an email address was provided)

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

## 4. Public Nature of Registrations

All subdomain registrations are made via pull requests to a **public GitHub repository** ([is-pinoy-dev/domains](https://github.com/is-pinoy-dev/domains)). By submitting a registration, you acknowledge that your GitHub username, subdomain name, DNS records, feature flags, and any optional fields you include (such as your email address) will be publicly visible in that repository and its history.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The service relies on the following third-party platforms, each governed by their own privacy policies:

- **GitHub** — pull request and repository hosting ([github.com/privacy](https://github.com/privacy))
- **Cloudflare** — DNS record hosting, traffic proxying (when `"proxied": true` is set on your record), Worker infrastructure for built-in tools, the aggregate traffic analytics we read for platform visit metrics, Turnstile bot verification and Email Routing for the Contact Form tool (see Section 5b) ([cloudflare.com/privacypolicy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/))

When your DNS record is proxied through Cloudflare, visitor traffic to your subdomain passes through Cloudflare's network. This is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy, not ours.

## 5a. Built-in Tools and Site Fetching

If you opt in to built-in tools (such as Site Audit), our infrastructure will make server-side HTTP requests to your subdomain to retrieve and analyze its content (HTML, metadata, and response headers). By enabling a tool, you consent to this. No data from your site's responses is stored beyond what is necessary to display results within the tool session.

We have no control over the data practices of third-party platforms listed above.

## 5b. Contact Form Tool

If you opt in to the Contact Form tool, a popup widget is added to your hosted portfolio. Visitors can submit their name, email address, and a message.

**Bot protection.** Submissions are verified with Cloudflare Turnstile before being accepted. Turnstile may process signals about the visitor's browser and device to distinguish humans from bots — see [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for what it collects.

**Messages are relayed, never stored.** A verified submission is emailed directly to your registered delivery address via Cloudflare Email Routing, with the visitor's own address set as the reply-to so you can respond directly. is-pinoy.dev does not store the message, the visitor's name, or the visitor's email address at any point — nothing about a submission persists on our side after delivery.

**Your delivery address.** To use this tool, you verify a delivery address in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.is-pinoy.dev/account). This address is your GitHub account's email. It is stored privately in our database (Cloudflare D1) and — unlike other optional subdomain fields — is **never** committed to the public [is-pinoy-dev/domains](https://github.com/is-pinoy-dev/domains) repository. It applies to every subdomain you own; you verify it once, not once per subdomain. Cloudflare requires you to confirm the address via a one-time email before it will deliver anything to it.

## 6. Platform Analytics

To monitor platform health and understand aggregate traffic patterns, we record daily visit totals for proxied subdomains on `*.is-pinoy.dev`.

**We do not observe individual visits.** Cloudflare measures traffic to proxied domains at its own edge and reports it to us only as aggregate counts. Once a day, a scheduled job reads the previous day's totals and stores them. There is no per-visit record on our side to collect, keep, or disclose.

**What we store, per subdomain per day:**

- The **subdomain name** (e.g. `juan` from `juan.is-pinoy.dev`)
- The **date** (a calendar day in UTC — not a per-visit timestamp)
- A **visit count** for that day
- The same count **broken down by visitor country**, as reported by Cloudflare's edge — no geolocation lookup is performed and no IP address ever reaches us

We do **not** receive or store paths, query strings, IP addresses, user-agent strings, referrers, session identifiers, or any other per-request detail at the platform level. The smallest unit of data we hold is one subdomain's total for one day.

**Where these totals are shown.** Your subdomain's total for the last 30 days appears on its card in the public [showcase](https://is-pinoy.dev/showcase) and on the landing page, alongside the subdomain name and the owner handle already published there. Only that single rounded figure and the window it covers are shown publicly — the per-day series and the country breakdown are visible to you alone, signed in to the [dashboard](https://dashboard.is-pinoy.dev/domains). A subdomain with no visits recorded shows no figure at all.

**This logging only applies to proxied subdomains.** If your DNS record has `"proxied": false` (DNS-only mode), your subdomain's traffic goes directly to your origin and is never intercepted by our infrastructure — no visit data is collected for DNS-only subdomains.

**You can opt out while staying proxied.** Set `"analytics": false` in your record's `features` block, or switch off *Visit analytics* for the subdomain in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.is-pinoy.dev/domains):

```json
"features": {
  "analytics": false
}
```

Cloudflare measures traffic to any proxied domain at its edge regardless of this setting, so the opt-out is applied where it can be honoured: our analytics job drops opted-out subdomains before any visit data is written to our database. Nothing for that subdomain is stored, and nothing is backfilled if you opt back in. Because the showcase can only display a figure we have stored, switching this off also removes the total from your card — the opt-out covers both the collection and the public display.

Because the totals are counted per hostname rather than per path, requests to platform tool paths on your subdomain (such as `/_tools/site-audit`) are not separated out and may be included in your subdomain's count.

Visit totals are stored in our own database (**Cloudflare D1**), and the underlying traffic measurement is Cloudflare's, subject to [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Data is used solely for aggregate platform reporting and is not shared, sold, or used for advertising purposes. This processing is carried out in compliance with the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**.

## 7. Data Retention

Your subdomain record is retained for as long as your subdomain remains active. If your subdomain is removed — by your request or by the maintainers — your DNS record will be deleted from the active configuration. Historical git commits in the public repository may still reference your username as part of the immutable repository history.

If you enabled the Contact Form tool, your verified delivery address is retained until you request its removal (open an issue at [github.com/is-pinoy-dev](https://github.com/is-pinoy-dev)) or it is erased under Section 8. Visitor-submitted message content is never retained in the first place, so there is nothing to delete on that side.

## 8. Your Rights

Under RA 10173, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request confirmation of what data we hold about you
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate information
- **Erasure** — request removal of your subdomain and associated data
- **Portability** — receive a copy of your data in a readable format
- **Object** — object to processing of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, open an issue at [github.com/is-pinoy-dev](https://github.com/is-pinoy-dev) with your request.

## 9. Security

Subdomain records are stored in a public GitHub repository. While we follow responsible open source practices, we cannot guarantee the security of publicly hosted data. Do not submit sensitive personal information. Your GitHub username and any optional fields you include (such as your email address) will be publicly visible.

## 10. Children's Privacy

This service is intended for developers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
